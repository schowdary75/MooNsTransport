# MooNsTransport Code of Conduct

MooNsTransport is built by people across different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone.

[📖 README](README.md) · [🤝 Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [👥 Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [⚖️ License](LICENSE) · [🛡️ Security](SECURITY.md)

---

## Expected Conduct

- **Be Respectful**: Treat collaborators and community members with dignity. Critique code and architecture, not the person.
- **Give Constructive Feedback**: Provide actionable feedback with clear rationale and reproduction steps.
- **Support Newcomers**: Welcome contributors of all skill levels and guide them without gatekeeping.
- **Safeguard Privacy & Security**: NEVER commit, share, or log sensitive information, real rider records, payment credentials, tokens, or personal identifiers.
- **Uphold Branding & Licensing**: Respect the MooNs branding and local evaluation license terms. Do not attempt to relicense or strip intellectual property notices.
- **Take Responsibility**: Acknowledge mistakes gracefully and work constructively toward resolution.
